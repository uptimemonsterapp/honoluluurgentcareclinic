Who We Are

Honolulu Urgent Care Clinic is a leading walk-in medical center located in the Ala Moana district of Honolulu, Hawaii. We are committed to providing fast, professional, and affordable healthcare to residents, visitors, and workers across Oahu.

Our clinic is equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic tools and staffed by board-certified physicians, physician assistants, and nurse practitioners who bring years of clinical experience to every patient interaction.

Our Mission & Values

We exist to make quality healthcare accessible, affordable, and convenient for every member of our community.

Compassionate Care

Every patient is treated with dignity, respect, and genuine concern for their well-being.

Fast Service

Most patients are seen within minutes of arrival — no long waits, no appointments required.

Clinical Excellence

Our providers follow evidence-based protocols to deliver accurate diagnoses and effective treatments.

Transparent Pricing

Affordable self-pay rates and most major insurance plans accepted — no surprise bills.

Why Choose Honolulu Urgent Care?

When you're not feeling well, the last thing you want is to spend hours in an emergency room waiting for care — or to wait days for a primary care appointment. That's where we come in.

We offer a comprehensive range of urgent care services, including treatment for neck pain, pink eye and conjunctivitis, pneumonia, skin rashes, sprains and strains, and urinary tract infections.

We also provide specialized services such as confidential STD screening and tuberculosis (TB) testing, making us a one-stop destination for your urgent healthcare needs.
